Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kampala University Nkobazambogo?

Kampala University Nkobazambogo is a community of students, members and leaders that works together for a stronger university community. This website is where members find news, events, election results and support.

How can I register as a member?

Select Register in the top menu, fill in your details and submit the form. You will be able to log in once your registration is confirmed.

How do I renew my membership?

Log in, open your profile and choose Renew Membership. If you have trouble, contact the support team.

Which payment methods are accepted?

The accepted payment methods are shown on the payment page when you pay. For help with a payment, use the Payment Support option in the Support Centre.

How can I vote in university elections?

Registered members can vote during the election period announced on the Leadership Elections page. Check that your registration is active before voting opens.

Where can I download university documents?

Election guidelines, the governance policy and the code of conduct are on the Leadership Elections page under Key Documents.

How do I contact the administration?

Use the Contact Us page, email info@nkobazambogo.ug or call +256 700 000 000 during office hours.

Is there a student membership category?

Yes. Students can register as members. Ask the support team about the categories that apply to you.

What details do I need to register?

You need your full name, a valid email address and a phone number. Some categories may ask for extra details.

I did not receive my confirmation. What should I do?

Check your spam folder first. If it is not there, contact the support team and we will help.

How do I pay my subscription?

Log in, open Payments and follow the steps shown. Keep your receipt for your records.

My payment did not go through. What now?

Do not pay twice. Contact Payment Support with the date, amount and your reference number.

Where can I see upcoming events?

Open the Events page from the top menu. It lists upcoming activities and past events.

Can I share photos from events?

Browse event photos on the Gallery page. To send in your own photos, contact the administration.

I cannot log in. What should I do?

Check your email and password, then use the Forgot Password link. If the problem continues, contact Technical Support.

The website is not loading properly.

Refresh the page or try another browser. If it keeps happening, report the problem through the Support Centre.

Where do I find user guides?

User guides and support documents are listed in Help & Resources in the Support Centre.

Where can I read the privacy policy and terms?

Both are on the Privacy Policy & Terms of Service page.

My question is not listed here.

Use the Contact Us page or start a Live Chat from the Support Centre, and the team will reply.
