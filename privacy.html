Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Draft text: have it reviewed before publishing.

Information we collect

When you register, contact us or use our website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, membership details and basic usage data such as pages visited.

How we use it

We use your information to manage your membership, respond to enquiries, share university news and events, run elections and keep our services secure.

Sharing

We never sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website, or where the law requires it.

Security

We use encryption, secure servers and limited access to protect your data. No system is completely risk-free, so please keep your password private.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@nkobazambogo.ug or +256 700 000 000.
